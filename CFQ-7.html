Cognitive Fusion Questionnaire, CFQ-7

Online version of the Cognitive Fusion Questionnaire (CFQ-7), a self-report measure assessing cognitive fusion – the extent to which individuals become entangled with their thoughts and treat them as literal truths. Within Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), cognitive fusion reflects a tendency for thoughts to strongly influence behavior and emotional responses, in contrast to defusion processes that promote psychological distance from internal experiences.
