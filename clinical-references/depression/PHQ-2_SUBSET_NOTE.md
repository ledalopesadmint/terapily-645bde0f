# PHQ-2 — Subset of PHQ-9

Items 1-2 of PHQ-9. See `depression/PHQ-9.pdf`.

- Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JBW (2003). The Patient Health Questionnaire-2: Validity of a Two-Item Depression Screener. Medical Care, 41(11), 1284-1292.
- Cutoff: ≥3 = positive screen → administer full PHQ-9
- Scoring: Sum of items 1-2 (range 0-6)
