# DBT-06 — Prós e Contras (Pros & Cons — Distress Tolerance)

## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Marsha M. Linehan, PhD
- **Source:** Linehan, M. M. (2015). *DBT Skills Training Manual.* 2nd Ed. Guilford Press. Module: Distress Tolerance. Handout 5: Pros and Cons.
- **Also:** Linehan, M. M. (2014). *DBT Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets.* 2nd Ed. Guilford Press. Worksheet 1 (Pros and Cons of Tolerating Distress).
- **Based on:** Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) — Linehan (1993)

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** drag_drop | **Sub-mode:** card_sort | **Duration:** 7-10 min

### Description
Patient evaluates a crisis behavior or urge by sorting consequences into a 2×2 matrix (4 quadrants):

|  | Acting on Urge | Resisting the Urge |
|--|---------------|-------------------|
| **Pros** | Short-term relief, perceived benefits | Long-term gains, self-respect |
| **Cons** | Long-term damage, guilt, escalation | Short-term discomfort, frustration |

The exercise builds distress tolerance by making implicit cost-benefit analysis explicit and visible.

### Clinical Use
- **Crisis prevention**: Completed in advance ("pre-loaded") so patient can reference during crisis.
- **Urge surfing support**: When urge is strong, patient reviews their own completed pros/cons.
- **Diary card integration**: Therapist reviews against DBT diary card entries.

### Standard DBT Protocol (Linehan, 2015)
1. Identify the crisis behavior or urge
2. List pros of acting on the urge
3. List cons of acting on the urge
4. List pros of resisting the urge
5. List cons of resisting the urge
6. Review: Which quadrant has the most compelling items?
7. Keep completed sheet accessible for crisis moments

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Therapist guides initial completion with a specific recent crisis/urge as the target.
- `shared_link`: Patient completes for homework — therapist reviews in next session.
- `both`: Complete in session with guidance, then patient re-does independently for different urge.

## Terapily Adaptation Notes
- **4 zones in 2×2 grid layout**: Prós de Agir, Contras de Agir, Prós de Resistir, Contras de Resistir.
- **Pre-written cards** with common consequences (e.g., "Relief in the moment," "Guilt afterward," "Proved I can handle it," "Frustration of waiting") — patient drags to appropriate quadrant.
- **Visual balance**: Card count per quadrant is visible, making the "weight" of each side tangible.
- **Data collected**: Quadrant distribution, which side "wins" (acting vs. resisting), specific items selected, completion time.
- **Clinical flags**: If patient loads all cards into "Pros of Acting" → flag for therapist (high crisis risk).
