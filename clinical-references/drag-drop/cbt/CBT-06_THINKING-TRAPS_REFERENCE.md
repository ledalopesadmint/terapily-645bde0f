# CBT-06 — Armadilhas de Pensamento (Thinking Traps — Simplified)

## Protocol Origin
- **Creators:** Dennis Greenberger, PhD & Christine A. Padesky, PhD
- **Source:** Greenberger, D. & Padesky, C. A. (2015). *Mind Over Mood: Change How You Feel by Changing the Way You Think.* 2nd Ed. Guilford Press. Chapter 6.
- **Based on:** Beck's cognitive distortion taxonomy, simplified for patient self-help and lower literacy populations.

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** drag_drop | **Sub-mode:** card_sort | **Duration:** 5-6 min

### Description
A simplified version of Burns' 10 cognitive distortions, reduced to 6 "thinking traps" that are more accessible for patients with less exposure to CBT concepts or lower health literacy. Patient sorts example thoughts into the correct trap category.

### The 6 Thinking Traps (Greenberger & Padesky)
1. **Tudo-ou-Nada** (All-or-Nothing) — "If it's not perfect, it's a failure."
2. **Catastrofização** (Catastrophizing) — "The worst thing will definitely happen."
3. **Leitura Mental** (Mind Reading) — "I know they think I'm stupid."
4. **Filtro Negativo** (Negative Filter) — Focusing only on what went wrong.
5. **Desqualificar o Positivo** (Discounting the Positive) — "That doesn't count."
6. **Deveria** (Should Statements) — "I should have known better."

### Clinical Use
- **Entry-level psychoeducation**: Ideal for patients new to CBT or with limited formal education.
- **Complementary to CBT-03**: Can be used as a simpler introductory version before the full Burns distortions exercise.
- **Self-help compatible**: Designed for independent use with minimal therapist guidance.

## Evidence Base
1. **Greenberger, D. & Padesky, C. A. (2015).** *Mind Over Mood.* 2nd Ed. Guilford Press. — Over 1 million copies sold; validated as effective self-help tool.
2. **Padesky, C. A. & Mooney, K. A. (1990).** Clinical tip: Presenting the cognitive model to clients. *International Cognitive Therapy Newsletter*, 6, 13-14. — Original simplified presentation method.
3. **Bieling, P. J., McCabe, R. E., & Antony, M. M. (2006).** *Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy in Groups.* Guilford Press. — Demonstrates simplified distortion categories in group CBT settings.
4. **Tolin, D. F. (2010).** Is cognitive–behavioral therapy more effective than other therapies? A meta-analytic review. *Clinical Psychology Review*, 30(6), 710-720. — Broad CBT efficacy supporting distortion identification interventions.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Therapist introduces each trap with patient-relevant examples before card sorting.
- `shared_link`: Effective as standalone homework — designed for self-help use.
- `both`: Standard flow — introduce in session, practice at home.

## Terapily Adaptation Notes
- **6 zones** matching the 6 traps (vs. CBT-03's Burns-derived categories).
- **Everyday language cards**: Examples use common daily situations, culturally neutral.
- **Differentiation from CBT-03**: CBT-06 is positioned as "introductory" while CBT-03 is "comprehensive." Therapist can choose based on patient profile.
- **Data collected**: Dominant trap, accuracy vs. reference key, time per card, blind spots.
