# CBT-05 — Ciclo Cognitivo-Comportamental (Cognitive-Behavioral Cycle)

## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Aaron T. Beck, MD (1976); updated by Judith S. Beck, PhD (2020)
- **Source:** Beck, A. T. (1976). *Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders.* International Universities Press.
- **Updated:** Beck, J. S. (2020). *Cognitive Behavior Therapy: Basics and Beyond.* 3rd Ed. Guilford Press. Chapter 2: The Cognitive Model.
- **Based on:** Beck's Cognitive Model — the foundational framework of all CBT

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** drag_drop | **Sub-mode:** cycle_builder | **Duration:** 8-12 min

### Description
The Cognitive Model describes how situations influence thoughts, which in turn affect emotions, physical sensations, and behaviors — forming a self-reinforcing cycle. Patient fills each slot of the cycle with their own personal experience.

### The Five-Component Cycle (Beck, J. S., 2020)
```
Situação (Trigger)
    ↓
Pensamento Automático (Automatic Thought)
    ↓
Emoção (Emotion)
    ↓
Resposta Fisiológica (Physical Response)
    ↓
Comportamento (Behavior)
    ↗ (feeds back to situation/thought)
```

### Clinical Use
- **Psychoeducation**: Core teaching tool in sessions 1-3 of standard CBT protocols.
- **Case conceptualization**: Therapist uses completed cycle to identify maintenance factors.
- **Pattern recognition**: Patient learns how thoughts drive the entire chain.

## Evidence Base
1. **Beck, A. T. (1963).** Thinking and depression: I. Idiosyncratic content and cognitive distortions. *Archives of General Psychiatry*, 9(4), 324-333. — Original cognitive model proposal.
2. **Beck, A. T. (1976).** *Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders.* International Universities Press. — Formalized the situation→thought→emotion→behavior cycle.
3. **Beck, J. S. (2020).** *Cognitive Behavior Therapy: Basics and Beyond.* 3rd Ed. Guilford Press. — Updated 5-component model with physiological responses.
4. **Dobson, K. S. & Dozois, D. J. A. (2019).** *Handbook of Cognitive-Behavioral Therapies.* 4th Ed. Guilford Press. — Comprehensive review of the cognitive model's evidence base.
5. **Hofmann, S. G., Asnaani, A., Vonk, I. J., Sawyer, A. T., & Fang, A. (2012).** The efficacy of cognitive behavioral therapy: A review of meta-analyses. *Cognitive Therapy and Research*, 36(5), 427-440. doi:10.1007/s10608-012-9476-1 — Meta-review confirming CBT model across 269 studies.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: **Recommended** for first use — therapist guides the patient through mapping a recent distressing event onto the cycle.
- `shared_link`: Patient can practice mapping additional situations at home.
- `both`: Teach in session, assign 2-3 homework cycles.

## Terapily Adaptation Notes
- **cycle_builder sub-mode**: 5 visual slots arranged in a circular layout. Patient drags cards into the correct position.
- **Two interaction modes** (configurable by therapist):
  1. **Recognition mode**: Pre-written cards → patient places in correct slot (psychoeducation).
  2. **Application mode**: Empty slots with prompts → patient writes their own content (deeper processing).
- **Visual design**: Circular flow with arrows showing the feedback loop. Each slot has a distinct color tint for quick identification.
- **Data collected**: Correct/incorrect placement (recognition mode), written content (application mode), completion time, which components patient struggled with most (empty or last-filled slots).
