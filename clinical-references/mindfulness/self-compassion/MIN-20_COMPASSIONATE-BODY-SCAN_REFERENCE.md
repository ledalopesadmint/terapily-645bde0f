# MIN-20 — Body Scan Compassivo (Compassionate Body Scan)
## Protocol Origin
- **Creators:** Kristin Neff, PhD & Christopher Germer, PhD — Mindful Self-Compassion (MSC) program
- **Source:** self-compassion.org — free audio + instructions
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 12 min
### Script Outline
Same progression as MBSR body scan, but each region receives appreciation:
1-8. Scan each body region (feet→head), but add: "Thank you, [body part], for all you do for me."
9. Closing: "May I accept my body as it is. May I appreciate all it does."
## Evidence Base
1. Neff KD, Germer CK (2013). A pilot study and RCT of MSC program. *JPSP*, 107(2), 178-192. — MSC reduced anxiety, depression, stress; increased self-compassion and life satisfaction.
2. Germer CK, Neff KD (2019). *Teaching the Mindful Self-Compassion Program.* Guilford Press.
3. Ferrari M, et al. (2019). Self-compassion interventions: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Mindfulness*, 10, 1455-1473. — Self-compassion interventions show moderate-to-large effects on well-being.
4. Neff KD, Germer CK. self-compassion.org — Compassionate Body Scan audio freely available.
