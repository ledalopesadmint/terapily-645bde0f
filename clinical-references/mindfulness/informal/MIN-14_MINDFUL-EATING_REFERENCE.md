# MIN-14 — Comer Consciente (Mindful Eating / Raisin Exercise)
## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Jon Kabat-Zinn, PhD — MBSR "Raisin Exercise" (1990)
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 8 min
### Script: 5 senses with a single piece of food (raisin, grape, chocolate)
1. See (1.5min) 2. Touch (1.5min) 3. Smell (1.5min) 4. Taste slowly (2min) 5. Reflect (1.5min)
## Evidence Base
1. Kabat-Zinn J (1990). *Full Catastrophe Living.* — Original raisin exercise.
2. Hong PY, et al. (2014). Mindful eating and raisin exercise. *Mindfulness*, 5, 80-87.
3. Dalen J, et al. (2010). Mindful eating and living (MEAL). *Complementary Therapies in Medicine*, 18(6), 260-264.
