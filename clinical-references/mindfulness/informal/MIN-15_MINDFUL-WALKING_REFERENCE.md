# MIN-15 — Caminhada Consciente (Mindful Walking)
## Protocol Origin
- **Sources:** MBSR (Kabat-Zinn), Thich Nhat Hanh, Jon Kabat-Zinn walking meditation protocol
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 10 min
### Steps: Walk slowly (10-15 paces), focus on feet contact, turn mindfully, repeat.
## Evidence Base
1. Kabat-Zinn J (1990). *Full Catastrophe Living.* — Walking meditation protocol.
2. Teut M, et al. (2013). Mindful walking in psychologically distressed individuals: RCT. *Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine*, 489856. PMC3747483. — Significant reduction in psychological distress.
3. Gotink RA, et al. (2016). Standardised MBSR and MBCT in adults: Systematic review and meta-analysis. *PLOS ONE*, 11(11), e0165681.
