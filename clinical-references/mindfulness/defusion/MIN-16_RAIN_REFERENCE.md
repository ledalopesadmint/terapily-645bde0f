# MIN-16 — RAIN (Recognize, Allow, Investigate, Nurture)
## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Tara Brach, PhD (adapted from Michele McDonald's original RAIN ~2005)
- **Updated acronym:** N = Nurture (Brach, 2020), replacing N = Non-identification
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 10 min
### 4 Steps
1. **Recognize** what is happening (1.5min)
2. **Allow** the experience to be there (2min)
3. **Investigate** with kindness — where in the body? (3min)
4. **Nurture** with self-compassion — what does this part need? (3.5min)
## Evidence Base
1. Brach T (2020). *Radical Compassion: Learning to Love Yourself and Your World with RAIN.* Penguin.
2. Neff KD, Germer CK (2013). A pilot study of MSC. *JPSP*, 107(2), 178-192. — Self-compassion practices (including RAIN) reduce anxiety/depression.
3. Brach T (2003). *Radical Acceptance.* Bantam. — Original framework.
