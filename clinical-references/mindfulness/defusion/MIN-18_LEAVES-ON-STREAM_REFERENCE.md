# MIN-18 — Folhas no Rio (Leaves on a Stream)
## Protocol Origin
- **Source:** ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) — Cognitive Defusion exercise
- **Author:** Russ Harris (2009), based on Hayes, Strosahl & Wilson (1999)
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 7 min
### Script Outline
1. Visualize sitting beside a gently flowing stream with leaves on the surface (1min)
2. Each thought that enters your mind — place it on a leaf and watch it float away (4min)
3. If you get "hooked" by a thought, notice that, and gently return to the stream (1min)
4. Return to the room (1min)
