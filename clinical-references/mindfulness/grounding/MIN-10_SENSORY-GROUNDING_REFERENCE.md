# MIN-10 — Grounding Sensorial (Body-Based Sensory Grounding)
## Protocol Origin
- **Source:** Trauma-informed care protocols; SAMHSA TIP 57 (2014)
- **Distinct from:** 5-4-3-2-1 (TRA-02) which uses external senses. This focuses on internal body sensations.
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 5 min
### Script Outline
1. **Feet (1.5 min):** "Press your feet firmly into the floor. Feel the weight. The temperature. The texture of your shoes or the floor."
2. **Hands (1.5 min):** "Press your palms together firmly. Notice the warmth. The pressure. Now release and feel the difference."
3. **Breath anchor (2 min):** "Place a hand on your belly. Feel it rise... and fall. You are here, in this room, right now."
## Evidence Base
