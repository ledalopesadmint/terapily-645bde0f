# MIN-09 — Lugar Seguro (Safe/Calm Place Visualization)
## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Francine Shapiro, PhD (EMDR Phase 2: Preparation)
- **Reference:** Shapiro F (2001, 2018). *EMDR: Basic Principles, Protocols, and Procedures.* Guilford Press. pp. 117-119.
- **Also used in:** Trauma-informed care, DBT (imagery), somatic experiencing
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 8 min
### Script Outline
1. **Invitation (1 min):** "Think of a place — real or imagined — where you feel completely safe and at peace."
2. **Sensory details (2 min):** "What do you see? Colors, light... What do you hear? What temperature is the air? What textures do you feel?"
3. **Emotional anchoring (2 min):** "Notice the feeling of safety in your body. Where do you feel it? Let it expand."
4. **Cue word (1 min):** "Choose a single word that captures this feeling. Say it to yourself."
5. **Deepening (1 min):** "Breathe into this feeling. Let it fill your whole body."
6. **Return (1 min):** "When you're ready, gently bring awareness back to the room."
## Evidence Base
1. Shapiro F (2001). *EMDR.* Guilford Press. — Original Safe Place Installation protocol.
2. Korn DL, Leeds AM (2002). Preliminary evidence of efficacy for EMDR resource development and installation. *Journal of Clinical Psychology*, 58(12), 1465-1487.
3. Jarero I, Artigas L (2012). The EMDR Integrative Group Treatment Protocol. *Journal of EMDR Practice and Research*, 6(4), 136-151.
4. ISTSS (International Society for Traumatic Stress Studies). Practice Guidelines for PTSD — recommends resource installation as preparation for trauma processing.
## Contraindications / Notes
- Some trauma survivors cannot access "safe" imagery — offer "calm" or "comfortable" as alternatives
- NOT a trauma processing technique — it's a stabilization/resource tool
