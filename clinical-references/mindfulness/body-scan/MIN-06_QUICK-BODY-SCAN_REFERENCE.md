# MIN-06 — Body Scan Rápido (3-Minute Body Scan)

## Protocol Origin
- **Derived from:** MBSR Body Scan (Kabat-Zinn, 1990)
- **Adaptation:** Brief clinical version for in-session use or acute moments
- **Similar to:** "Brief Body Scan" in MBCT (Segal, Williams & Teasdale, 2002)

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 3 min

### Script Outline
1. **Anchor (30s):** Close eyes or lower gaze. Take one deep breath. Feel your feet on the floor.
2. **Three anchors (2 min):**
   - **Feet (40s):** Notice the soles of your feet. Weight, warmth, contact with the floor.
   - **Hands (40s):** Notice your hands. Are they warm? Cool? Tingling? Just notice.
   - **Breath (40s):** Bring attention to the belly. Feel it rise and fall. Stay here.
3. **Return (30s):** Gently expand awareness outward. Notice sounds. Open eyes when ready.

**Key instruction:** "Three anchors — feet, hands, breath. When in doubt, come back to one of these."

## Evidence Base
1. **Kabat-Zinn J (1990).** *Full Catastrophe Living.* — Original body scan protocol; abbreviated versions used in clinical practice since inception.
2. **Segal ZV, Williams JMG, Teasdale JD (2002).** *Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression.* Guilford Press. — MBCT includes abbreviated body scans as "informal practice."
3. **Deng X, et al. (2022).** The effects of body scan meditation: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Mindfulness*, 13, 1792-1811. — Even brief body scans (< 5 min) showed significant anxiolytic effects.
4. **Carmody J, Baer RA (2009).** How long does a mindfulness-based stress reduction program need to be? *Mindfulness*, 1, 67-73. — Brief practices can produce meaningful benefits when practiced consistently.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Ideal para início de sessão ou após material emocionalmente carregado
- `shared_link`: Prática diária rápida. Ideal para pacientes que "não têm tempo"
- `both`: Recomendado

## Contraindications / Notes
- Mesmas precauções trauma-informed do body scan completo
- Versão mais acessível para pacientes resistentes a meditação longa
- Pode ser usado como "reset" durante o dia
