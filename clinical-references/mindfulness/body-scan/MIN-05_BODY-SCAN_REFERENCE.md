# MIN-05 — Body Scan (Varredura Corporal)

## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Jon Kabat-Zinn, PhD — University of Massachusetts Medical School
- **Program:** MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction), established 1979
- **Status:** Core practice of MBSR — first exercise taught in the 8-week program

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 15 min (abbreviated version; traditional = 30-45 min)

### Script Outline (Abbreviated Clinical Version)
1. **Setup (1 min):** Lie down or sit comfortably. Close eyes gently. Take 3 deep breaths.
2. **Feet (2 min):** Bring attention to the soles of the feet. Notice sensations — warmth, pressure, tingling. No need to change anything, just notice.
3. **Legs (2 min):** Move attention up through ankles, calves, knees, thighs. Notice weight, temperature, tension.
4. **Pelvis & abdomen (2 min):** Observe the rise and fall of the belly. Notice any sensations without judgment.
5. **Back & spine (2 min):** Scan from lower back upward. Notice contact with the surface beneath.
6. **Chest & shoulders (2 min):** Notice breathing in the chest. Observe shoulder tension without fixing it.
7. **Hands & arms (2 min):** Fingers, palms, wrists, forearms, upper arms. Notice subtle sensations.
8. **Neck, face & head (2 min):** Jaw, cheeks, eyes, forehead, scalp. Soften any held tension.
9. **Whole body integration (1 min):** Expand awareness to encompass the entire body. Breathe with the whole body. Gently open eyes.

**Key instruction:** "If you notice your mind has wandered, that's perfectly normal. Simply notice where it went, and gently return attention to the body."

## Evidence Base
1. **Kabat-Zinn J (1990).** *Full Catastrophe Living: Using the Wisdom of Your Body and Mind to Face Stress, Pain, and Illness.* Delta/Bantam Books. — Original protocol.
2. **Ussher M, et al. (2014).** The MBSR Body Scan in Clinical Practice. *Mindfulness*, 4, 394-401. Springer. DOI: 10.1007/s12671-013-0212-z. — Clinical adaptations and evidence for shortened versions.
3. **Deng X, et al. (2022).** The effects of body scan meditation: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Mindfulness*, 13, 1792-1811. PubMed: 35538557. — Meta-analysis: body scan significantly reduces anxiety (d = 0.44) and improves body awareness.
4. **Schmalzl L, Powers C, Henje Blom E (2015).** Neurophysiological and neurocognitive mechanisms underlying the effects of yoga-based practices. *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 9, 235. — Body scan activates interoceptive cortex (insula), improving mind-body connection.
5. **Colgan DD, et al. (2019).** Body scan and mindful breathing among veterans with PTSD. *Mindfulness*, 10(8), 1669-1680. PMC7451147. — Body scan comparable to mindful breathing for PTSD depression symptoms in veterans.

## Delivery Modes

- `both`: Recomendado — introduzir em sessão, prescrever versão guiada para casa.

## Contraindications / Notes
- **Trauma-informed adaptation:** Para sobreviventes de trauma, oferecer opção "olhos abertos" e "pular áreas do corpo que causem desconforto"
- Pode evocar emoções fortes — normalizar isso como parte do processo
- Versão curta (15 min) é preferida em contexto clínico; versão completa (45 min) é do MBSR original
