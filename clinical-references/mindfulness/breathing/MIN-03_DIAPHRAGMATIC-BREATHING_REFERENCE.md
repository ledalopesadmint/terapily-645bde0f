# MIN-03 — Respiração Diafragmática (Diaphragmatic Breathing)

## Protocol Origin
- **Clinical basis:** Foundation of MBSR (Kabat-Zinn, 1990), CBT anxiety protocols, pulmonary rehab
- **Also known as:** Belly breathing, abdominal breathing, deep breathing
- **History:** Documented in clinical literature since 1970s; MBSR formalized use in 1990

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 5 min (10-12 breath cycles)

### Steps
1. Sit or lie comfortably. Place one hand on chest, one on abdomen.
2. Inhale slowly through the nose for **4 counts** — abdomen rises, chest stays still
3. Pause briefly (1-2 counts)
4. Exhale slowly through the mouth for **6 counts** — abdomen falls
5. Repeat for 5 minutes
6. Focus on the hand on the abdomen rising and falling

**Key instruction:** The longer exhale (4:6 ratio) maximizes parasympathetic activation. Chest hand should not move significantly.

## Evidence Base
1. **Hopper SI, Murray SL, Ferrara LR, Singleton JK (2019).** Effectiveness of diaphragmatic breathing for reducing physiological and psychological stress in adults. *JBI Database of Systematic Reviews*, 17(9), 1855-1876. — Systematic review: DB significantly reduces cortisol, blood pressure, respiratory rate, and subjective stress.
2. **Ma X, et al. (2017).** The effect of diaphragmatic breathing on attention, negative affect and stress in healthy adults. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 8, 874. — RCT: 8 weeks of DB reduced cortisol and improved sustained attention.
3. **Hamasaki H (2020).** Effects of diaphragmatic breathing on health: A narrative review. *Medicines*, 7(10), 65. MDPI. — Comprehensive review: DB improves cardiorespiratory fitness, reduces anxiety, and is safe for clinical populations.
4. **Kabat-Zinn J (1990).** *Full Catastrophe Living: Using the Wisdom of Your Body and Mind to Face Stress, Pain, and Illness.* Delta/Bantam Books. — Original MBSR protocol, DB as foundational practice.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Ideal para ensinar a mecânica (mão no peito vs abdômen)
- `shared_link`: Prática diária com timer e instrução visual
- `both`: Padrão recomendado

## Contraindications / Notes
- Seguro para quase todas as populações, incluindo DPOC e cardíacos (com orientação)
- Pode causar tontura leve em iniciantes — orientar "pare se sentir desconforto"
- Fundação de todas as outras técnicas de respiração
