# MIN-02 — Respiração Quadrada (Box Breathing / Tactical Breathing)

## Protocol Origin
- **Popularized by:** Mark Divine, former Navy SEAL commander (SEALFIT)
- **Clinical adaptation:** Used in VA/DoD stress management programs, first responder training
- **Roots:** Square breathing pattern documented in yoga (Sama Vritti Pranayama)

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 4 min (4-6 cycles)

### Steps
1. Inhale through the nose for **4 counts**
2. Hold breath (lungs full) for **4 counts**
3. Exhale through the mouth for **4 counts**
4. Hold breath (lungs empty) for **4 counts**
5. Repeat 4-6 cycles

**Key instruction:** Equal-ratio breathing (4:4:4:4). Can adjust to 5:5:5:5 or 6:6:6:6 for experienced practitioners.

## Evidence Base
1. **Balban MY, Neri E, Kogon MM, et al. (2023).** Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. *Cell Reports Medicine*, 4(1), 100895. — Stanford RCT: 5-min daily breathwork > mindfulness meditation for mood and arousal reduction.
2. **Zaccaro A, et al. (2018).** How breath-control can change your life: A systematic review on psycho-physiological correlates of slow breathing. *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 12, 353. — Slow breathing activates vagus nerve, shifts ANS toward parasympathetic.
3. **Röttger S, et al. (2021).** Square breathing influences on cognitive performance. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*, 46, 207–219. — Box breathing improved working memory and reduced subjective stress.
4. **Cleveland Clinic (2021).** How Box Breathing Can Help You Destress. health.clevelandclinic.org. — Clinical overview: "simple but powerful" for stress management.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Excelente para abertura de sessão ou após exposição
- `shared_link`: Timer visual ideal para prática diária em casa
- `both`: Padrão recomendado

## Contraindications / Notes
- Seguro para população geral
- Usado por militares, primeiro-socorristas, e atletas de elite
- Adaptação clínica: ratio 4:4:4:4 pode ser reduzido para 3:3:3:3 em pacientes ansiosos
