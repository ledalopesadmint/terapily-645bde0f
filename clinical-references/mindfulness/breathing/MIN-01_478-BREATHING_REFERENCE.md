# MIN-01 — Respiração 4-7-8 (4-7-8 Breathing)

## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Andrew Weil, MD — University of Arizona Center for Integrative Medicine
- **Based on:** Pranayama breathing (yogic tradition), adapted for clinical Western use
- **Year:** Popularized ~2010; Weil has taught since 1990s

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 5 min (4 cycles standard, expandable to 8)

### Steps
1. Exhale completely through the mouth with a whooshing sound
2. Inhale quietly through the nose for **4 counts**
3. Hold breath for **7 counts**
4. Exhale completely through the mouth (whoosh) for **8 counts**
5. Repeat cycle 4 times (beginners) → 8 times (advanced)

**Key instruction:** The ratio 4:7:8 matters more than absolute speed. Start with 4 cycles, 2x/day.

## Evidence Base
1. **Vierra J, et al. (2022).** Effects of sleep deprivation and 4-7-8 breathing control on heart rate variability in healthy young adults. *Physiological Reports*, 10(13), e15389. PMC9277512. — Showed 4-7-8 improved HRV during sleep deprivation.
2. **Ma X, et al. (2017).** The effect of diaphragmatic breathing on attention, negative affect and stress in healthy adults. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 8, 874. — Controlled breathing protocols reduce cortisol and improve sustained attention.
3. **Weil A. (2015).** *Breathing: The Master Key to Self-Healing.* Sounds True. — Original clinical protocol description.
4. **Zaccaro A, et al. (2018).** How breath-control can change your life: A systematic review on psycho-physiological correlates of slow breathing. *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 12, 353. — Meta-review: slow breathing (< 10 breaths/min) activates parasympathetic nervous system.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Terapeuta guia contagem ao vivo. Ideal para introdução.
- `shared_link`: Paciente pratica em casa com timer visual. Homework clássico.
- `both`: Mais comum — ensina em sessão, prescreve para casa.

## Contraindications / Notes
- Não recomendado durante crise de pânico aguda (a pausa de 7s pode aumentar ansiedade)
- Seguro para população geral; sem riscos documentados para 4 ciclos
- Weil recomenda máximo de 4 ciclos no primeiro mês
