# MIN-17 — S.T.O.P. (Stop, Take a Breath, Observe, Proceed)
## Protocol Origin
- **Sources:** DBT Emotion Regulation (Linehan, 2015) + MBCT/MBSR informal practice
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 3 min
### 4 Steps
1. **Stop** — Pause whatever you're doing (30s)
2. **Take a breath** — One conscious breath (30s)
3. **Observe** — Thoughts, feelings, body sensations, environment (1min)
4. **Proceed** — Continue with awareness (1min)
## Evidence Base
1. Linehan MM (2015). *DBT Skills Training Manual, 2nd Ed.* Guilford Press. — STOP skill in Emotion Regulation module.
2. Segal ZV, Williams JMG, Teasdale JD (2002). *MBCT for Depression.* — STOP as informal mindfulness bridge.
