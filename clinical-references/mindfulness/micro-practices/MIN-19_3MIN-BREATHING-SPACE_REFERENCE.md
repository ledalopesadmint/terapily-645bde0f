# MIN-19 — Espaço de Respiração de 3 Minutos (Three-Minute Breathing Space)
## Protocol Origin
- **Creators:** Zindel Segal, Mark Williams, John Teasdale — MBCT (2002)
- **Status:** Signature practice of MBCT — "the most important practice in the program"
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 3 min (1 min per phase)
### 3 Phases (Hourglass Shape)
1. **Awareness (1 min):** "What is my experience right now?" Thoughts, feelings, body sensations. Wide lens.
2. **Gathering (1 min):** Narrow focus to the breath at the belly. Anchor attention.
3. **Expanding (1 min):** Expand awareness to the whole body. Sense of the body breathing. Carry this awareness forward.
## Evidence Base
1. Segal ZV, Williams JMG, Teasdale JD (2002). *MBCT for Depression.* Guilford Press. — Original protocol.
2. Crane R (2009). *Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy.* Routledge. Ch. 23: The Three-Minute Breathing Space.
3. Kuyken W, et al. (2016). Efficacy of MBCT in prevention of depressive relapse. *JAMA Psychiatry*, 73(6), 565-574. — MBCT (including 3MBS) as effective as antidepressants for relapse prevention.
