# MIN-07 — Relaxamento Muscular Progressivo (Progressive Muscle Relaxation — PMR)

## Protocol Origin
- **Creator:** Edmund Jacobson, MD, PhD (1934) — original 16-group protocol
- **Clinical adaptation:** Bernstein & Borkovec (1973) — simplified to 16→7→4 muscle groups
- **Further refined:** Wolpe (1958) for systematic desensitization; Öst (1987) for applied relaxation

## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 12 min (8-group version)

### 8-Group Protocol (Bernstein & Borkovec adaptation)
For each group: TENSE for **5 seconds** → RELEASE and notice relaxation for **15-20 seconds**

1. **Hands & forearms:** Make fists, tense forearms → release
2. **Upper arms (biceps):** Flex biceps → release
3. **Forehead:** Raise eyebrows high → release
4. **Face (center):** Scrunch nose, squeeze eyes → release
5. **Jaw & neck:** Clench jaw, press tongue to roof → release; press chin to chest → release
6. **Shoulders & upper back:** Shrug shoulders to ears → release
7. **Abdomen:** Tighten stomach muscles → release
8. **Legs & feet:** Press toes down, tighten calves and thighs → release

**Key instruction:** "Focus on the CONTRAST between tension and relaxation. That contrast teaches your body what relaxation feels like."

## Evidence Base
1. **Jacobson E (1938).** *Progressive Relaxation.* University of Chicago Press. — Original 200+ page protocol.
2. **Bernstein DA, Borkovec TD (1973).** *Progressive Relaxation Training: A Manual for the Helping Professions.* Research Press. — Simplified protocol that became the clinical standard.
3. **Manzoni GM, et al. (2008).** Relaxation training for anxiety: A ten-years systematic review with meta-analysis. *BMC Psychiatry*, 8, 41. — PMR effective for anxiety (moderate-to-large effect sizes) across multiple conditions.
4. **Toussaint L, et al. (2021).** Effectiveness of PMR in adults for stress, anxiety, and depression: A systematic review. *Psychology Research and Behavior Management*, 17, 345-365. — Recent comprehensive review confirming efficacy for GAD, insomnia, chronic pain.
5. **Li Y, et al. (2025).** Progressive muscle relaxation technique improves sleep quality and mental health: A systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs. *PubMed*: 41633054. — Latest meta-analysis: PMR significantly improves sleep quality (SMD = 0.87).
6. **APA Division 12 (Society of Clinical Psychology).** Lists PMR as "strong research support" for generalized anxiety disorder. div12.org.

## Delivery Modes
- `in_session`: Terapeuta guia cada grupo muscular com voz calma. Essential para aprender.
- `shared_link`: Timer visual com instruções por grupo + contagem de tensão/relaxamento.
- `both`: Altamente recomendado — técnica precisa ser ensinada corretamente.

## Contraindications / Notes
- **Dor crônica:** Evitar tensionar grupos musculares com dor ativa — usar "release-only" (Conrad & Roth, 2007)
- **Hipertensão:** Monitorar; tensão isométrica pode elevar PA brevemente
- **Lesão muscular:** Pular grupos afetados
- Gold standard para GAD (Borkovec et al., 1987) — recomendado por NICE e APA
