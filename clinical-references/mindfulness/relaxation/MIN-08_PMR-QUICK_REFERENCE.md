# MIN-08 — PMR Rápido (5-Group Quick PMR)
## Protocol Origin
- **Derived from:** Bernstein & Borkovec (1973) 16→7→4 group progression
- **Clinical use:** Abbreviated version for homework and time-limited sessions
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 6 min (5 groups)
### 5-Group Protocol
Tense **5s** → Release **15s** each:
1. Both hands + forearms (make fists)
2. Both arms (flex biceps)
3. Face (scrunch everything)
4. Shoulders + neck (shrug + chin to chest)
5. Legs + feet (press toes, tighten all)
## Evidence Base
1. Bernstein DA, Borkovec TD (1973). *Progressive Relaxation Training.* Research Press.
2. Öst LG (1987). Applied relaxation: Description of a coping technique. *Behaviour Research and Therapy*, 25(5), 397-409. — Abbreviated PMR as effective as full version for maintenance.
3. Manzoni GM, et al. (2008). Relaxation training for anxiety. *BMC Psychiatry*, 8, 41.
