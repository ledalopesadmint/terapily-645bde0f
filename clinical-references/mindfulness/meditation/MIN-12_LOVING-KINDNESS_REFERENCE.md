# MIN-12 — Meditação de Bondade Amorosa (Loving-Kindness / Metta Meditation)
## Protocol Origin
- **Tradition:** Buddhist Metta Bhavana, adapted for clinical use
- **Clinical adaptation:** Sharon Salzberg (1995), Kristin Neff & Christopher Germer (MSC program)
- **Used in:** MBCT, CFT (Compassion-Focused Therapy), MSC (Mindful Self-Compassion)
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_script | **Duration:** 10 min
### Script Outline
1. **Self (2 min):** Bring yourself to mind. Repeat silently: "May I be safe. May I be healthy. May I be happy. May I live with ease."
2. **Loved one (2 min):** Bring to mind someone you care about. Direct the same phrases toward them.
3. **Neutral person (2 min):** Someone you neither like nor dislike (cashier, neighbor). Same phrases.
4. **Difficult person (2 min):** Someone with whom you have mild difficulty (start small). Same phrases. (Optional — can skip initially.)
5. **All beings (2 min):** Expand outward: "May all beings be safe. May all beings be healthy. May all beings be happy. May all beings live with ease."
## Evidence Base
1. Hofmann SG, Grossman P, Hinton DE (2011). Loving-kindness and compassion meditation: Potential for psychological interventions. *Clinical Psychology Review*, 31(7), 1126-1132. PMC3176989. — Review: LKM increases positive emotions and reduces negative affect.
2. Fredrickson BL, et al. (2008). Open hearts build lives: Positive emotions, induced through loving-kindness meditation, build consequential personal resources. *JPSP*, 95(5), 1045-1062. — RCT: LKM produced increases in daily positive emotions.
3. Galante J, et al. (2014). Effect of kindness-based meditation on health and well-being. *JPSP*. — Systematic review: moderate evidence for depression, positive emotions.
4. Shahar B, et al. (2015). A wait-list RCT of LKM for self-criticism. *Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapy*, 22(4), 346-356. — LKM reduced self-criticism and depression in self-critical individuals.
5. Kearney DJ, et al. (2013). Loving-kindness meditation for PTSD: A pilot study. *Journal of Traumatic Stress*, 26(4), 426-434. — Veterans showed reduced PTSD and depression symptoms.
## Contraindications / Notes
- "Difficult person" step is OPTIONAL and should start with mild difficulty
- Some trauma survivors find self-directed kindness triggering — normalize this
- Phrases can be adapted: "May I feel safe" instead of "May I be safe"
