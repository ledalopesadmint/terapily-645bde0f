# MIN-11 — Respiração Consciente (Mindful Breathing / Breath Awareness)
## Protocol Origin
- **Source:** MBSR (Kabat-Zinn, 1990) and MBCT (Segal, Williams & Teasdale, 2002)
- **Status:** The foundational formal meditation practice in both MBSR and MBCT
## Clinical Protocol
**Type:** guided_timer | **Duration:** 10 min
### Steps
1. Sit in a comfortable, upright position. Close eyes or lower gaze.
2. Bring attention to the natural breath — don't change it, just observe.
3. Notice where you feel the breath most vividly (nostrils, chest, or belly).
4. Stay with that anchor point. Breathe naturally.
5. When the mind wanders (it will), gently note "thinking" and return to breath.
6. Bell/chime marks end. Take a moment before opening eyes.
## Evidence Base
1. Kabat-Zinn J (1990). *Full Catastrophe Living.* Delta/Bantam Books.
2. Segal ZV, Williams JMG, Teasdale JD (2002). *MBCT for Depression.* Guilford Press.
3. Balban MY, et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood. *Cell Reports Medicine*, 4(1), 100895. — Stanford RCT: daily 5-min breathwork improves mood.
4. Goyal M, et al. (2014). Meditation programs for psychological stress and well-being: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *JAMA Internal Medicine*, 174(3), 357-368. — Gold-standard review: meditation programs showed moderate evidence for anxiety, depression, pain.
