# Cognitive Fusion Questionnaire (CFQ-7)

## Citation
Gillanders DT, Bolderston H, Bond FW, et al. (2014). The development and initial validation of the Cognitive Fusion Questionnaire. Behavior Therapy, 45(1), 83-101.

## Access
- DOI: 10.1016/j.beth.2013.09.001
- PubMed: 24411117
- contextualscience.org (free)
- Free for clinical/research use

## Items: 7
Rated 1 (never true) to 7 (always true)

1. My thoughts cause me distress or emotional pain.
2. I get so caught up in my thoughts that I am unable to do the things that I most want to do.
3. I over-analyze situations to the point where it's unhelpful to me.
4. I struggle with my thoughts.
5. I get upset with myself for having certain thoughts.
6. I tend to get very entangled in my thoughts.
7. It's such a struggle to let go of upsetting thoughts even when I know that letting go would be helpful.

## Scoring
- Total: Sum all items (range 7-49)
- No reverse items
- Cutoffs: 7-21 Low fusion | 22-35 Moderate | 36-49 High cognitive fusion
