# Work and Social Adjustment Scale (WSAS)

## Citation
Mundt JC, Marks IM, Shear MK, Greist JH (2002). The Work and Social Adjustment Scale: A simple measure of impairment in functioning. British Journal of Psychiatry, 180(5), 461-464.

## Access
- DOI: 10.1192/bjp.180.5.461
- PubMed: 11983645
- Free for clinical/research use

## Items: 5
Rated 0 (not at all) to 8 (very severely)

Rate how much your [problem] impairs your ability to carry out each activity:

1. WORK — ability to work (or study, manage home, volunteer, etc.)
2. HOME MANAGEMENT — cleaning, tidying, shopping, cooking, looking after home/children, paying bills
3. SOCIAL LEISURE ACTIVITIES — social leisure activities with other people (parties, pubs, outings, entertaining, etc.)
4. PRIVATE LEISURE ACTIVITIES — private leisure activities done alone (reading, gardening, walking, etc.)
5. CLOSE RELATIONSHIPS — ability to form and maintain close relationships with others including those I live with

## Scoring
- Total: Sum all items (range 0-40)
- Cutoffs: 0-9 Low impairment | 10-19 Moderate (significant but less severe) | 20-40 Moderately severe to severe (suggests change from treatment possible)
