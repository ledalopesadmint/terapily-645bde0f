# GAD-2 — Subset of GAD-7

Items 1-2 of GAD-7. See `anxiety/GAD-7.pdf`.

- Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JBW, Monahan PO, Löwe B (2007). Anxiety Disorders in Primary Care: Prevalence, Impairment, Comorbidity, and Detection. Annals of Internal Medicine, 146(5), 317-325.
- Cutoff: ≥3 = positive screen → administer full GAD-7
- Scoring: Sum of items 1-2 (range 0-6)
