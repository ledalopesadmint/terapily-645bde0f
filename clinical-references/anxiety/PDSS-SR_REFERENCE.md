# Panic Disorder Severity Scale — Self-Report (PDSS-SR)

## Citation
Houck PR, Spiegel DA, Shear MK, Rucci P (2002). Reliability of the self-report version of the Panic Disorder Severity Scale. Depression and Anxiety, 15(4), 183-185.

Original clinician version:
Shear MK, Brown TA, Barlow DH, et al. (1997). Multicenter collaborative panic disorder severity scale. American Journal of Psychiatry, 154(11), 1571-1575.

## Access
- PubMed: 12112724 (self-report), 9356566 (original)
- Free for clinical/research use

## Items: 7
Rated 0-4

1. Frequency of panic attacks (0=none, 4=nearly every day)
2. Distress during panic attacks (0=none, 4=extreme)
3. Severity of anticipatory anxiety (0=none, 4=extreme/pervasive)
4. Agoraphobic fear/avoidance (0=none, 4=extreme/pervasive)
5. Interoceptive fear/avoidance (0=none, 4=extreme/pervasive)
6. Impairment in work functioning (0=none, 4=extreme)
7. Impairment in social functioning (0=none, 4=extreme)

## Scoring
- Total: Sum all items (range 0-28)
- Cutoffs: 0-1 None | 2-5 Borderline | 6-9 Slightly ill | 10-13 Moderately ill | 14-16 Markedly ill | ≥17 Severely ill
