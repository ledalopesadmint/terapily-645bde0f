# Relapse Prevention Plan — Reference Document

## Original Source
- **Authors:** G. Alan Marlatt, PhD & Judith R. Gordon, PhD
- **Publication:** Marlatt GA, Gordon JR (1985). *Relapse Prevention: Maintenance Strategies in the Treatment of Addictive Behaviors.* Guilford Press.
- **Updated:** Marlatt GA, Donovan DM (2005). *Relapse Prevention: Maintenance Strategies in the Treatment of Addictive Behaviors, Second Edition.* Guilford Press.

## Why No PDF
The original Marlatt & Gordon (1985) format is not published as a free standalone worksheet.
Multiple free adaptations exist (Therapist Aid, SimplePractice, PsychPoint, SAMHSA) but
none is the canonical "original." The structure below is the evidence-based consensus format.

## Canonical Structure (6 Sections)

### 1. Personal Triggers
- Internal triggers (emotions, thoughts, physical sensations)
- External triggers (people, places, situations, times)
- High-risk situations identified

### 2. Warning Signs
- Behavioral changes (isolation, routine disruption)
- Emotional changes (irritability, hopelessness)
- Cognitive changes (romanticizing use, minimizing consequences)
- Physical signs (sleep disruption, appetite changes)

### 3. Coping Strategies
- Immediate coping (distraction, grounding, delay tactics)
- Cognitive coping (challenging thoughts, pros/cons review)
- Behavioral coping (exercise, hobbies, routine maintenance)
- Emotional regulation (mindfulness, breathing, journaling)

### 4. Support Network
- Name, relationship, phone number (3-5 contacts)
- Sponsor/mentor contact
- Professional contacts (therapist, psychiatrist, crisis line)
- Support group schedule

### 5. Emergency Plan
- Crisis hotline numbers (988, local)
- Emergency contacts
- Steps if relapse occurs (no shame, contact therapist, attend meeting)
- Nearest treatment facility

### 6. Personal Motivation
- Reasons for recovery (top 5)
- Goals for the future
- Values alignment (what matters most)
- Review schedule (weekly/monthly)
